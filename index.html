System 01 / Roster

The most dangerous roster, on the record.

Right now your roster is photos on a scroll. Give every member a real profile: game stats, socials, achievements, and a tier badge, auto-updated from live APIs.

  • Three tracks for Pros, Creators, and Streamers, each with the stats that matter.
  • Live-pulled follower counts, stream status, and tournament placements.
  • Tier badges (Pro, Core, Creator, Prospect) that tie straight into The Grind.
  • Sponsor-ready: one link that proves the roster's reach in numbers.
System 02 / Compete

You win $10K events. Show the world in real time.

Host your own 7VEN cups or track your pros in outside events. Auto-seeded brackets, live scores, and a permanent results hub that turns every win into content and sponsor proof.

  • Run 7VEN tournaments end to end: sign-ups, brackets, check-ins, payouts.
  • Live scorekeeping the community can watch update in real time.
  • A results archive so your wins are a page, not a buried tweet.
  • Per-player stats that flow back into roster profiles.
System 03 / Recruit

The recruitment engine. This is the one.

You said it yourself: you want hungry people who are reliable, active, and ready to build. Stop guessing who those are. The Grind is a gamified tryout pipeline that measures it automatically, always running.

  • Tracks what matters: Discord activity, stream hours, and social posts, pulled automatically.
  • Daily and weekly quests keep prospects grinding and coming back.
  • A tier ladder (Prospect to Grinder to Trial to 7VEN) with auto-promotion.
  • A live leaderboard so the hungriest rise to where you can see them.
System 04 / Community

Turn 43K watchers into 43K participants.

Attention is worthless if it just scrolls. The Vote gives the community real skin in the game, and every vote is an engagement spike and a reason to come back.

  • The community picks the next member off the Grind leaderboard.
  • Clip of the week and monthly MVP, voted by verified fans.
  • Every poll is a content drop and a follower magnet.
  • Anti-botting baked in: verified members only.

System 05 / Content

Creator Studio: thumbnails and clips on autopilot.

Your creators live and die on output. Give them tools that make 7VEN-branded content in seconds and push it everywhere, with no editor babysitting.

  • Thumbnail generator: on-brand 7VEN thumbnails from a title and screenshot.
  • Auto-clipper bot: watches streams, cuts the highlights, drops them ready to post.
  • Post pipeline: clip to review to scheduled across TikTok, YT, X, and IG.
  • Every asset stamped with the brand. Consistent, always.
System 06 / Control

Everyone sees their game. You see the whole board.

Every member gets a dashboard: their stats, grind points, tasks, and growth. You get the command view (who's active, who's slipping, what's landing) so you run 7VEN on data, not vibes.

  • Member view: personal stats, quests, clip queue, earnings.
  • Owner view: org-wide activity, growth, and content performance.
  • Powered by the 7VEN Discord bot: roles, intake, tracking, votes, automated.
  • Alerts when a prospect is heating up or a member is going quiet.
